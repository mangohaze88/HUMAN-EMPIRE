## What's Inside?

### 8 Cutting-Edge Methods Analyzed

1. **Forward-Forward Algorithm** (Hinton 2022+)
   - 84.7% CIFAR-10 accuracy without backprop
   - Layer-local learning, hardware friendly
   - Implementation: 2 weeks

2. **Predictive Coding Networks**
   - Hierarchical prediction errors
   - No separate backward pass
   - Implementation: 2 weeks

3. **Equilibrium Propagation**
   - Energy-based learning, 4 orders of magnitude energy savings
   - Hardware-native (analog circuits)
   - Implementation: 1 month

4. **Dendritic Computation**
   - 2025 breakthrough: Biological evidence of vectorized learning
   - Multi-compartment neurons solve credit assignment
   - Implementation: 3-4 weeks

5. **STDP (Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity)**
   - Millisecond temporal precision
   - Native support on Intel Loihi 2
   - Implementation: 3 weeks

6. **Neuromorphic Hardware** (Loihi 2, NorthPole, Hala Point)
   - 1000x energy efficiency vs GPUs
   - 1 billion neurons (Hala Point)
   - Deployment: 1-2 months

7. **Thousand Brains Theory** (Numenta 2024)
   - Few-shot learning, compositional generalization
   - Open-source Monty framework
   - Implementation: 6-8 weeks

8. **Active Inference** (Free Energy Principle)
   - Unified perception-action-learning framework
   - Curiosity-driven exploration
   - Implementation: 6-8 weeks

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which method should I implement first?**
A: Forward-Forward Algorithm. Easiest (2 weeks), highest impact (20-30% improvement), perfect fit with stigmergic architecture.

**Q: Do I need neuromorphic hardware?**
A: No for Phase 1-3. Develop on CPU/GPU first. Loihi deployment in Phase 2 is optional but recommended for energy validation.

**Q: How does this compare to backpropagation?**
A: Modern bio-plausible methods achieve 80-95% of backprop accuracy with 2-5x more samples, but 1000x better energy efficiency on neuromorphic hardware.

**Q: Is this production-ready?**
A: Phase 1-2 methods (Forward-Forward, Predictive Coding, STDP, Loihi) are production-ready. Phase 3-4 are research extensions.

**Q: What about transformers/LLMs?**
A: These methods are for fully local learning. Transformers need global attention. Future work: Bio-plausible attention mechanisms.
